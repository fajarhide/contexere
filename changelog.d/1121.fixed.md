- **npm, OpenAI, Stripe and Google keys are redacted wherever they appear (#1121, #1112)**: the bare-token patterns knew Anthropic, GitHub, AWS, Slack and JWT shapes only, so an `npm_`, `sk-proj-`, `sk_live_` or `AIza` key with no secret-named key beside it reached the model. The four shapes are now bare-token patterns at their issuer's length.
