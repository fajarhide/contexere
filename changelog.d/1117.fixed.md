- **A redacted quoted `export` keeps the command after it (#1117, #1126, #1128)**: `export DATABASE_PASSWORD="v"; next` lost everything after the closing quote, with no marker, because the redaction ran from `=` to the end of the line. The tail is now kept when the value's end is certain: an unescaped closing quote followed by whitespace, `;`, `&` or `|`, and no further assignment after it, so `"[REDACTED]"; next` keeps `; next`. A backtick after the quote, an escaped quote, a glued or joined segment (`"a"b`, `"a"" b"`), a second assignment on the line, and every unquoted value are still hidden to the end of the line, since each can carry part of the secret. `` `export KEY="v"` `` wrapped in backticks was not redacted at all and now is.
