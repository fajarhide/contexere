- **A redacted quoted `export` keeps the rest of its line (#1117, #1126, #1128)**: `export DATABASE_PASSWORD="v"` followed by a backtick or `;` lost everything after the closing quote, with no marker, because the redaction ran from `=` to the end of the line. The tail is now kept when the value's end is certain: an unescaped closing quote followed by whitespace, an operator, or a backtick that closes one opened earlier on the line, and no further assignment after it. So `"[REDACTED]"; tail` keeps `; tail`. An escaped quote, a glued or joined segment (`"a"b`, `"a"" b"`), a backtick that may open a command substitution, a second assignment on the line, and every unquoted value are still hidden to the end of the line. `` `export KEY="v"` `` wrapped in backticks was not redacted at all and now is. Seen on `gh issue list`, where a title quoting such a line came back cut.
