- **Typing `omni` at a terminal says it is now `contexere` (#876)**: the old name runs the same binary and keeps working, and until now nothing told a person they were on it. One grey line on stderr now does, and points at `contexere doctor`, which lists every hook and setting still on the old name. Stdout never changes, and the line is printed only when stderr is a terminal, so a hook or an MCP client sees nothing new; a `cmd | omni` typed at a terminal still shows it.
