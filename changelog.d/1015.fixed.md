- **Output that mentions secrets in its first line is no longer blanked (#1015)**: 0.8.3 read any first line with a comma and the word "secrets" as a CSV header, so all the output after it came back as `[REDACTED]` with a count of hidden values, even on a public README. A secret column now needs a short name, and most lines after the header have to hold the delimiter. A real table with an odd row, or a secret holding a comma, is still hidden.
