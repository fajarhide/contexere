- **The pre-hook refused commands that only named a credential source as text (#1130)**: `grep -c` with the pattern `docker inspect`, a `gh issue create` whose title mentioned it, and `cat > notes.md` with a quoted heredoc whose body quoted it or `cat ./.env` were all refused as if they would print the source. The `grep -c` case is the inspection the refusal itself recommends. Two causes: the remote-source check matched the two words anywhere in a stage, and every line of a heredoc body was read as a command, as was the pattern after any `-c` (`grep -c "cat .env"`). A source now counts only in command position (first after wrappers, under `ssh`, after `--`, as the script of a shell's `-c`, or in a backtick or `$(` substitution outside single quotes), and the body of a quoted heredoc is skipped unless the line feeds a shell, an interpreter or `ssh`. `timeout`, `xargs`, `watch`, `doas` and `stdbuf` are now read as wrappers, so `timeout 5 docker inspect web` is still refused.
