- **A number under a name that describes a credential is delivered (#1022, #1009)**: `token_limit = 100000`, `token_count = 12` and `models_noauth=401` were `[REDACTED]`. A key with a segment such as `LIMIT`, `COUNT`, `MAX`, `TTL`, `STATUS` or `NOAUTH`, holding an integer or an expression that starts with one, now passes. `APP_TOKEN=123`, `DB_AUTH=302` and `AUTH_CODE=123456` stay hidden, and so does a bare `auth=200`.
