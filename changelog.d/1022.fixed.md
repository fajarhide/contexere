- **A number under a name that describes a credential is delivered (#1022, #1009)**: `token_limit = 100000 if p else 8000`, `token_count = 12` and `models_noauth=401` came back `[REDACTED]`, because the key matched `TOKEN` or `AUTH` and the value was never looked at. A key with a segment such as `LIMIT`, `COUNT`, `MAX`, `TTL`, `STATUS` or `NOAUTH` now passes when it holds an integer, alone or followed by a conditional, a comment or arithmetic; on the built binary both issue repros deliver their values. A word after the number, a second bare number, or an assignment, field or long literal in the comment keeps it hidden, so `MAX_PASSWORD="123 correct horse"` and `token_limit = 12 # API_TOKEN=x` stay redacted. `APP_TOKEN=123`, `DB_AUTH=302`, `AUTH_CODE=123456` and a bare `auth=200` stay hidden too, per the review of #865.
