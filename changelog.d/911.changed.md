The ledger stores each command once instead of on every line it showed. On a 30-day store that took `ledger_lines` from 420 MB to 50 MB and the whole `omni.db` from 615 MB to 262 MB, measured on a rebuilt copy. Rows written before the upgrade keep their text until they age out of the 30-day window, so the saving arrives as that window turns over; a `VACUUM` after that returns the freed pages to the disk.
