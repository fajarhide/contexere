- **A path to a secret was hidden as if it were the secret (#950)**: `KEY=/etc/app/backup_ed25519` and `TOKEN_FILE=/run/secrets/api_token` came back as `[REDACTED]`, which removed the one fact a runbook or script review needs. An absolute or `~/` path is now delivered when it has two segments or more, path characters only (no `+`, `=`, `:`, `@` or space) and no segment of 12 characters or more mixing upper, lower and digits, so a base64 secret that opens with `/` stays hidden. Replayed over 56,504 recorded lines holding `=`: 15 stopped being redacted, every one a `*_FILE` or `KEY` path, and none became newly redacted.
